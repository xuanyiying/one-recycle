# OneRecycle Product Overview

OneRecycle is a multi-platform recycling mini-program that allows users to submit old item recycling orders and schedule door-to-door pickup services across WeChat, Alipay, TikTok, and Kuaishou platforms.

## Core Features
- **Multi-platform support**: WeChat, Alipay, TikTok, Kuaishou mini-programs
- **Item recycling**: 10+ categories including clothing, electronics, books, gold, etc.
- **Door-to-door service**: Courier pickup scheduling and management
- **Price estimation**: Real-time pricing for recyclable items
- **Order management**: Complete order lifecycle tracking
- **Admin dashboard**: Web-based management interface

## Business Model
- Users submit recycling orders through mini-programs
- Couriers are dispatched for pickup
- Items are evaluated and priced on-site
- Payments processed through integrated payment systems
- Admin staff manage operations through web dashboard

## Target Users
- **End users**: Individuals looking to recycle old items for cash
- **Couriers**: Service providers handling pickups and evaluations
- **Administrators**: Staff managing operations, users, and system settings
